What Is AURA?

AURA is an intelligent safety ecosystem designed to transform camera infrastructure into active monitoring networks. It aims to identify potential incidents, verify events, and coordinate alerts through a centralized dashboard.


The system is being developed for environments where timely awareness and coordinated response can support safety teams.
