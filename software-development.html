Clean Architecture Standard

Decoupled, Testable, and Bulletproof Codebases

We build enterprise software that eliminates technical debt. By strictly enforcing Clean Architecture and Domain-Driven Design (DDD), your core business logic remains independent of external frameworks, databases, and UI layers.

  • Event-Driven Messaging: Kafka, RabbitMQ, and gRPC for sub-millisecond asynchronous communication.
  • Fault Tolerance: Circuit breakers, exponential backoff, and idempotent transaction boundaries.
  • Zero-Downtime Migration: Strangler Fig pattern for non-disruptive legacy modernization.
  • Comprehensive Testing: Unit, integration, mutation, and end-to-end load tests exceeding 90% coverage.
