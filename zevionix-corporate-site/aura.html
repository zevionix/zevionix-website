AURA: intelligent safety. Coordinated response.

AI Emergency Response & Smart Campus System — a developing platform designed to connect visual event detection with incident verification, response alerts, and centralized monitoring.

Turning camera infrastructure into an active safety network.

AURA explores how computer vision and event coordination can help campus teams improve situational awareness when a possible emergency occurs.

The system is being developed around a workflow that detects a potential incident, verifies it, notifies designated responders, and displays status through a dashboard.

AURA is an in-development project. Detection accuracy, deployment readiness, and operational performance require further testing and validation.
