ZEVIONIX AURA Core Platform

The unified autonomous cognitive layer that turns distributed video streams, sensor telemetry, and business events into self-optimizing operations.

Autonomous Intelligence

Perception, Reasoning, and Action in One Unified Mesh

AURA (Autonomous Unified Reasoning Architecture) is an enterprise AI engine engineered for environments requiring sub-millisecond decisions, air-gapped zero-trust security, and massive horizontal scaling.

  • Universal Vision Ingest: Processes 100+ concurrent RTSP/CSI camera feeds with real-time YOLOv10 spatial parsing.
  • Autonomous Agent Swarms: Coordinated reasoning agents that detect operational drifts and execute remediations.
  • Edge-to-Cloud Federation: Syncs lightweight quantized models to edge gateways with central model orchestration.
  • Zero-Trust Cryptographic Vault: End-to-end payload encryption (AES-256-GCM) and hardware enclave security.

Platform Architecture

The Four Pillars of AURA

Built to be deployed on-premises in air-gapped hardware enclaves or as a multi-tenant cloud cluster.

AURA Vision

Real-time spatial computing, automated bounding telemetry, sub-millimeter anomaly detection, and facial/object classification.

AURA AgentMesh

Self-coordinating LLM and reasoning agent swarms that orchestrate complex multi-step remediation workflows without human delay.

AURA StreamPulse

High-throughput real-time telemetry engine capable of querying 10M+ metrics/sec using columnar in-memory indexes.

AURA Shield

Confidential computing hardware enclave protection for proprietary weights, tenant isolation, and immutable audit logging.
