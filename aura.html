Edge Privacy & Data Governance Architecture

Zevionix Zero-Trust Memory Isolation Policy

When activating the Live Webcam testing mode, your browser requests a local sandboxed video stream. All spatial inference runs strictly in client memory:

  • Pure In-Memory Processing: Raw frames reside only in temporary GPU/CPU display buffers and are immediately released after each frame.
  • Zero Server Ingestion: No image, video feed, audio track, or biometric embedding is sent over the network or saved to remote databases.
  • Hardware Sensor Release: Switching back to demo presets or closing the tab invokes track.stop(), turning the physical camera off immediately.
  • Simulated Air-Gapped Edge Node: Mirrors the zero-trust hardware enclave model implemented in AURA production edge installations.
