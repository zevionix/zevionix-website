Frequently Answered Questions

Clarifying Architecture Questions

Everything you need to know about intellectual property, legacy integration, and engineering engagements.

How does Zevionix integrate with legacy databases (e.g. Oracle Banner, SAP S/4HANA) without downtime?
We utilize Debezium Change Data Capture (CDC) and strangler fig facade proxies. Rather than directly executing heavy analytical queries against your production transactional database, we asynchronously capture row-level changes via transaction logs and stream them into isolated Apache Kafka brokers. Your core business operations continue at 100% velocity with zero locks.
Who retains intellectual property rights for custom code and trained model weights?
All proprietary application code, custom machine learning model weights, data pipelines, and architectural blueprints developed for your engagement are 100% owned by your organization upon milestone completion. We execute strict Mutual NDAs before any technical briefing.
How do you handle strict compliance regulations like FERPA, HIPAA, or PCI-DSS?
Our architectures are designed from day one with zero-trust confidential boundaries. We employ hardware-enclave data anonymization, tokenization pipelines, role-based access control (RBAC), and automated audit logs. Sensitive workloads can be deployed in fully air-gapped on-premise infrastructure or isolated sovereign cloud zones.
What happens when edge nodes lose internet connectivity in transit or industrial facilities?
Our edge daemons (built in Rust and C++) feature local embedded RocksDB storage capable of caching up to 72 hours of sensor telemetry and inference events offline. Once WAN connectivity resumes, transactions are automatically validated, cryptographically hashed, and deduplicated into central cloud clusters.
What is the typical timeframe from initial discovery to a deployed production pilot?
Following our 4-stage engineering lifecycle, an initial Architectural Discovery and micro-Proof-of-Concept is typically delivered within 3 to 4 weeks. Staged production canary rollouts usually take between 6 to 10 weeks depending on integration complexity.
