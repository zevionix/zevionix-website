Frequently Asked Questions

Clarifying Answers for Technical Leaders

Understand how Zevionix Technologies approaches collaboration, system architecture, intellectual property, and enterprise execution.

How does Zevionix Technologies structure engineering engagements?
We offer dedicated engineering squads, milestone-based turnkey project delivery, and co-development models. Every engagement begins with an architectural discovery sprint to define API contracts, non-functional requirements, security postures, and delivery milestones.
What makes AURA distinct from standard video management systems?
Standard video management systems require human monitoring and passive recording. AURA leverages edge-accelerated computer vision models (such as custom YOLO and Vision Transformers) to actively detect, verify, and coordinate automated alerts for critical events like falls or unauthorized breaches in real time.
How does the strategic partnership with Coralgenz Global benefit projects?
Our strategic collaboration with Coralgenz Global expands our deep-tech research velocity, grants access to specialized international delivery resources, and ensures access to cutting-edge compiler, security, and enterprise infrastructure technologies.
Can Zevionix modernize legacy monolithic enterprise systems?
Yes. We specialize in decoupling legacy monoliths into cloud-native microservices using strangler fig patterns, event-driven streaming with Apache Kafka, automated containerization with Docker/Kubernetes, and modern frontend design systems.
How is intellectual property and codebase ownership handled?
All proprietary code, trained custom model weights, architectural blueprints, and artifacts developed for client engagements are 100% owned by the client upon completion with strict NDA and confidentiality guarantees.
