Introducing AURA

AI Emergency Response & Smart Campus System

Intelligent Safety. Faster Response. Smarter Environments.

AURA is an AI-powered emergency response ecosystem designed to transform existing camera infrastructure into active safety networks.


By combining computer vision, event verification, and automated incident coordination, AURA aims to reduce response delays and improve situational awareness across campuses and commercial facilities.


The platform is being developed to support real-time incident detection, verification, alert dispatch, and centralized monitoring.
